01 / About the project

Making knowledge-grounded AI more trustworthy

Large language models are transforming how we access, interpret and generate information. Yet their reliability depends on their ability to retrieve and integrate accurate knowledge.

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) combines language models with external knowledge sources to improve factual accuracy and relevance. EVALLA aims to advance the evaluation of these systems by developing a clear and standardized framework for assessing effectiveness, reliability and knowledge integration. Rigorous evaluation can help build more transparent, trustworthy and useful AI systems.

02 / Research

Research focus

A rigorous approach to evaluating retrieval-augmented large language models.

01 / RETRIEVAL

Knowledge retrieval

Assessing how effectively RAG systems find and use relevant information from external sources.

02 / RELIABILITY

Factual reliability

Evaluating answer quality, factual grounding and the trustworthiness of generated responses.

03 / EVALUATION

Standardized evaluation

Developing a clear framework to measure effectiveness and knowledge integration consistently.
